About the survey

Employees Federal Credit Union surveyed members to learn how they viewed its services and operations, including why they might borrow from other institutions.

  • This analysis uses 128 responses from the cleaned Module 10 dataset.
  • The results cover R1, R2, R5, and R6.
  • "No opinion" and "No answer" responses are excluded where appropriate.
